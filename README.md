# AWACS – Clinical Medicine Contribution

Student contribution to Team AWACS (Astronaut Wellness Monitoring System), an exploratory wellness-support prototype for the NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026 (November 14–15, 2026).

## Live demo (sample data)
Dashboard built by Jude (Team AWACS):
https://site-805353b925304a718040254659b2f894.freebuff.page/

## My role
Clinical medicine (medical student). I worked on:
- User stories and priorities for the clinical alerts
- Plain-language risk explanations based on NASA Human Research Program risks (cardiovascular, immune, sensorimotor, vision, bone, behavioural health, radiation)
- Draft zero-gravity procedure checklists, each marked "requires clinical review"
- Medical user acceptance testing and a clinical fix list for the prototype

## Team
Brett Gallie (team lead, business analysis), Jude (software), Rahaf Almutairi (pharmacy), Noor Fatima (clinical medicine)

## Important notice
All data is sample data. This is a student project, not a medical device and not clinical guidance. Checklists and thresholds are drafts that require review by a physician or pharmacist before any use.
